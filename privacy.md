# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

SocialHub is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own social media accounts.

**What data it uses.** When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram), SocialHub receives an access token and basic profile and statistics data for that account (name, follower and view counts). It uses this only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform.

**Where it is stored.** Everything stays on the owner's own computer. SocialHub has no server, no database in the cloud and no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

**Third parties.** Videos and captions are sent only to the platform they are posted on (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram), under that platform's own privacy policy.

**Deleting data.** Disconnecting an account in SocialHub deletes its stored token. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own account settings.

**Contact.** Open an issue on this repository.
