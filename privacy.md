# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

SocialHub is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

**What data it uses.** When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram), SocialHub receives an access token and basic profile and statistics data for that account (channel or account name, follower, view and like counts of the owner's own videos). It uses this only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform.

**YouTube API Services.** SocialHub uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read those channels' own statistics. By using SocialHub with YouTube you also agree to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). SocialHub requests only these permissions: upload videos (`youtube.upload`) and read the channel's own data (`youtube.readonly`). It does not access, store or show data of other YouTube users.

**Where it is stored.** Everything stays on the owner's own computer. SocialHub has no server, no cloud database, no analytics and no cookies or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

**Third parties.** Videos and captions are sent only to the platform they are posted on (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram), under that platform's own privacy policy.

**Keeping and deleting data.** While an account is connected, its statistics are refreshed from the platform every few hours. Disconnecting an account in SocialHub deletes its stored token and the statistics received from that platform, and stops all access. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings; for Google/YouTube at the [Google security settings page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). If access is revoked there, SocialHub can no longer read anything and the owner disconnects the account in SocialHub to delete what was stored.

**Contact.** Open an issue on this repository.
